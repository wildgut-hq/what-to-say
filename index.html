Read this bit

The two rules everything else follows from

  1. You are not the person who decides if it is safe for someone else. Not for their medication, not for their condition, not for their pregnancy. The answer is always that they check with their GP or pharmacist. Saying so makes you the trustworthy one, not the unhelpful one.
  2. Never tell anyone to change, stop or skip something a doctor gave them. Not in a video, not in a comment, not in a DM. No exceptions, no matter how the question is phrased.

The core of it

Say this, not that

Most videos that get pulled are pulled over a handful of words. Swapping them costs you nothing and keeps your account clean.

Don't saySay insteadWhy
It fixes it · cures · treats · heals "It supports my digestion" · "it keeps me moving" · "it helps me stay regular" Those are medical claims. We support, we do not treat.
It helped me lose weight Leave weight out of it. The story is comfort and being regular. This is the single biggest cause of violations on our product. See the last section.
Clinically proven "The ingredients are backed by research" We cannot say the finished product is clinically proven.
Detox · cleanse · flush "Support" · "keep things moving" Banned for us. It is also the language that dates the product.
It's safe with your medication "Check with your prescriber or pharmacist before adding anything new" You cannot make a safety call on someone else's prescription. Do not take that on.
My gut journey Just say what happened. "Journey" is a banned word across everything WildGut.
Ozempic · Wegovy · Mounjaro "my GLP-1" · "the injection" · "weight loss injection" Brand drug names get flagged and rejected automatically.
Kilos of waste · pounds of build-up "Backed up" · "things weren't moving" · "felt heavy" It reads as a weight claim, and it is exactly the kind of line that gets pulled.
14 day cleanse · a course · finish the pack and you're done "It's a daily habit" There is no finish line. People stay on it.

When to stop and escalate

  • Someone describes severe pain, vomiting, blood, or not having gone for over a week. Tell them to speak to their GP or pharmacist. Do not recommend the product into that.
  • Someone asks whether they should stay on or come off a prescription. Not your call and not ours.
  • Anyone mentions a reaction they think came from the product. Send it straight to us, same day.
  • A question about allergens or an interaction that you cannot answer from this page.
  • You get a violation you do not think you deserved. We have a TikTok Shop rep who can look at your account and get it overturned.
Message Humza directly on WhatsApp for anything urgent, or email support@wildgut.co.uk. Better to ask and wait than to guess in a comment.

Background

Why the weight rule matters most

Of everything on this page, that is the one worth understanding rather than just following.

The situationWhat it means for you
Supplements and weight loss claims together are one of the most heavily policed combinations on the platform. It is the fastest route to a violation on this product, and violations land on your account rather than ours.
We do not put ad spend behind any video that talks about weight. So a weight video will not get boosted. You lose the ad-driven commission on it as well as taking the risk.
We work with a small selected group of creators rather than mass sending samples out. Violations across our retained creators are low, and sticking to this page is most of the reason why.
